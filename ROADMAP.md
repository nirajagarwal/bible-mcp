## Done (v0.11, August 2026 — Greek Septuagint, get_interlinear parity)
- [x] **Greek LXX text ingested** (`LXX-GRC`, Swete edition, Cambridge 1887–1894, via github.com/nathans/lxx-swete / First1KGreek — CC BY-SA 4.0, not the print edition's own PD status): 28,122 verses, 566,387 words, Genesis–Malachi + Apocrypha, Theodotion Daniel/Susanna/Bel. Reused the Brenton Psalms-remap logic directly (same underlying LXX structure, confirmed by re-deriving and matching) and hit the same Ezra/Nehemiah "combined Esdras B" split, this time without a separately-numbered Nehemiah to fall back on (renumbered chs 11–23 → 1–13 directly).
  - **Licensing ruled out first, not assumed**: Rahlfs (the more standard critical edition) was considered and rejected — the CCAT electronic text requires a signed, address-and-name user-registration agreement that binds the *signer* to gate every downstream recipient too, structurally incompatible with an unauthenticated public server no matter how permissive the non-commercial terms are. A third-party CC-BY-NC-SA repackaging of the same CCAT data doesn't clean up that chain of title. Raw OCR of the public-domain 1887–1894 print scans was also checked directly and found unusable (misread the English title page as Greek gibberish) before falling back to the clean First1KGreek transcription.
  - **Real bug caught by verification, not assumed correct**: the source's book-number field isn't zero-padded (`1.1.1`, not `01.1.1`) — an overly-strict regex silently dropped every line in Genesis through Judges (most of the Torah) on the first ingestion attempt. Caught by systematically diffing verse counts against BSB/WEB across every book rather than spot-checking a few, the same method that caught the original Brenton bugs.
  - One dedup fallback added for a genuine source quirk: Song of Songs 7 relabels mid-file around the classic LXX/English chapter 7-8 boundary split — one real ref collision, disambiguated rather than silently dropped.
  - Ecclesiastes, Odes, Psalms of Solomon, and the Old Greek (non-Theodotion) variants of Daniel/Susanna/Bel not ingested this pass — deferred, see Next.
- [x] **`get_interlinear` extended for LXX parity** — OT verses now show Hebrew/Aramaic (existing) *and* Septuagint Greek (new, surface form only) side by side when both exist; Apocrypha-only books (no Hebrew Vorlage in this corpus) show LXX Greek alone, which required fixing an early-continue that would have silently skipped them. Confirmed no regression: `get_cross_references`/`get_citations` filter `links` by an explicit `type=`, so the new `concept_*` types don't leak into their output (spot-checked against Rom 5:12 and Eph 5:21 — identical results before/after). Design recorded in DESIGN.md §5. New `scripts/build_concepts.py`, run incrementally against the existing db (not a fresh `build_db.py` rebuild).
- [x] **Easton's Bible Dictionary ingested** (`data/sources/theographic-json/easton.json`, already in the repo, sourced but never used until now) as its own `EASTON` document (2,491 entries, addressed `EASTON.{seq}.1` since the source has no native chapter/verse structure) — searchable/embeddable like every other layer. Grouped the source's flat per-paragraph rows by `termID` (an entry can span several `itemNum` rows, e.g. Covenant's 8-paragraph entry) rather than treating each paragraph as its own record.
- [x] **2,491 concept nodes seeded** from entries with at least one `matchType='unmatched'` row — i.e. not already a person/place entity (Covenant, Sacrifice, Sabbath, Holiness, Sin, Kingdom of God, Sanctification, ...). Inline markdown scripture links in `dictText` (e.g. `[Ex. 6:20](/exod#Exod.6.20)`) parsed directly into OSIS verse anchors — **13,085 OT-filtered `concept_mentions`** across 2,060 concepts (431 concepts deferred, no OT anchor found — mostly NT-only Easton entries, not discarded).
- [x] **3,182 keyword nodes** linked from concepts via exact match between a concept's label and a word's normalized `words.gloss` (e.g. Covenant → H1285 *berith*, G1242 *diatheke*; Sabbath → H7676 *shabbath*, G4521 *sabbaton*) — 3,475 `concept_derived_from` edges. **Bug caught before it shipped**: matching against raw `words.gloss` alone spuriously linked "Covenant" to H1886a (the Hebrew *definite article* — morph `Td`) and "Age" 's own keyword slot to H2050c (a pronominal suffix — morph `S`), because MACULA occasionally attaches a compound contextual gloss like `the.covenant` to a grammatical-particle row rather than the noun itself; stripping "the" left "covenant" keyed to the wrong Strong's number entirely. Fixed by restricting candidate strongs to their *majority* morph category (Noun/Verb/Adjective/Adverb only) before gloss-matching — caught by spot-checking the actual linked entries against their own labels, not just checking that *a* link came back (same verification habit as the v0.12 BDB homograph catch).
- [x] **84,472 mechanical `concept_associated` edges** (7,016 same-verse co-occurrence + 77,456 via chaining through the existing `cross_reference` links — reused, not re-derived) and **22,578 `concept_instance_of` edges** (entity → concept, via shared verse mentions with the existing `entity_mentions` table) — all weighted by overlap count, all `source`-tagged by method, zero LLM involvement. Verse-level fan-out capped at 30 concepts to keep an over-cited verse from generating combinatorial noise. Spot-checked: Covenant's top cross-reference-chain partners are Prophecy, Pentateuch, Circumcision, Christ — thematically sound; its `concept_instance_of` entities are God (weight 24), Abraham, Israel, David, Joshua — correct.

New `scripts/phase5_prepare.py` (gathers each pair's real evidence — both concepts' Easton definitions plus the actual cross-referenced verse text) and `scripts/phase5_apply.py` (writes verdicts back, logs every judgment to `outputs/concept-build-log-2026-09-26.jsonl`, no LLM API wired up — the pass ran in-session against real evidence, held to the same fixed-vocabulary-plus-cited-evidence rule an API-driven version would need). Reclassified the 6 pairs from the first batch that had been correctly, honestly left `associated` only because the vocabulary had no slot for them (no new evidence consulted — same citations, `outputs/phase5-judgments-2026-09-26-vocab-extension.json`). `scripts/phase5_apply.py` now handles both new verbs generically for future batches.
- [x] **Phase 5 completed for the full weight≥10 pool**: 823 additional `concept_associated` edges (weight 10-22, the full remainder after the first 50), processed in five ~150-220-pair chunks (`scripts/phase5_manual_chunk1.py`–`chunk5.py`), each pair judged against its own real evidence (definitions + cited verse text) and logged. Combined with the first batch: **879 total judgments**, **145 sharpened** (97 `broader`/`narrower` pairs, 26 `causes`, 46 `part_of`, 2 `symbol_of`, 1 `contrasts`), **734 correctly kept `associated`**. Regression-checked throughout (`cross_reference`/`citation` counts unchanged at every checkpoint).
  - **First real `contrasts` use**: Barren/Child (Gen 16:2's reproach of barrenness vs. Ps 127:3's "children are a heritage" — a genuine theological antonym pair, not forced onto weaker candidates like Baldness/Hair or Dress/Naked, which stayed `associated` on the honest judgment that removing something isn't the same as thematic opposition).
  - **A second vocabulary gap found and left alone, on purpose**: many pairs are near-synonyms or alternate names (Captivity/Exile, Edom/Seir, Bonnet/Mitre, Hades/Hell, Book/Roll) that the current five sharp verbs (`broader`/`narrower`/`causes`/`part_of`/`symbol_of`) have no clean slot for either — a `same_as` verb is a plausible future addition, deliberately not added this pass (unlike `part_of`/`symbol_of`, which were added because the *first* batch's evidence made the gap undeniable; this one can wait for a second data point).
  - **Real tooling bug found**: Phase 4's candidate generation creates one `links` row per (method, pair) rather than per pair, so a pair with both a `coverse` and a `cross_reference_chain` hit can appear twice in one static batch file at different weights (found once: Deuteronomy/Pentateuch, w=19 and w=11). Handled safely this run (the second occurrence was a no-op once the first was processed) but the next mechanical-edge-generation pass should `GROUP BY` the pair and merge/max the weight instead of emitting separate rows.
  - **A dozen-plus candidate pairs had zero recoverable evidence** at apply time (e.g. Passover/Sacrifice, Sabbath/Week, Ark/Divination) despite passing Phase 4's weight threshold — the candidate-weight computation and the evidence-reconstruction query in `phase5_prepare.py` disagree in a small number of cases; all were correctly left `associated` per the no-evidence-no-upgrade rule rather than guessed, but the discrepancy itself is worth root-causing before the next run.
- [x] **Phase 6 run**: a stratified random sample of 40 typed edges (15 `narrower`, 10 `causes`, 12 `part_of`, both `symbol_of` edges, the one `contrasts` edge — `scripts/phase6_sample.py`, seed=42, reproducible), independently re-verified against evidence re-fetched fresh from the db (not the stored justification text). Result: **32/40 confirmed clean, 7/40 plausible-but-thin evidence, 1/40 genuinely wrong**.
  - **The one error, found and fixed**: Adder→Poison and (once checked) Asp→Poison were both marked `narrower` on flawed reasoning — an adder is a creature that *has/produces* poison (venom), not a specific *instance* of poison the way Gall and Hemlock (poisonous plants) genuinely are. Gall/Poison and Hemlock/Poison stayed `narrower` (correctly — verified separately); Asp/Poison and Adder/Poison were reverted to `associated` and the correction logged in `concept-build-log-2026-09-26.jsonl`. This is exactly the class of mistake Phase 6 exists to catch: a plausible-sounding pattern-match (four "specific poisonous X" pairs judged in the same sitting) applied to two members that didn't actually fit the pattern.
  - **The 7 "plausible-but-thin" cases cluster in `causes`** (4 of 10 sampled): Loan→Debt, Agriculture→Food, Agriculture→Wine, Mourn→Hair. In each, the underlying claim is true, but the specific verse Phase 6's independent re-fetch turned up was weaker or more tangential than the verse the original Phase 5 judgment actually cited — a sign that `causes` judgments deserve more scrutiny than `narrower`/`part_of`/`symbol_of` (which came back 13/15, 10/12, and 2/2 clean respectively) in any future validation pass.
