# Composer notes — Hidden and Revealed

## Survey's own arc (reverse-engineered)
Sathar/galah core vocabulary and the jurisdictional Deut 29:29 split → galah's uncover/exile double-life → Isaiah 45 holding self-concealment and plain speech unresolved → Daniel 2's convergence of all three roots and the "Revealer of Mysteries" title → wisdom-literature's disagreement about hidden wisdom (Prov virtue / Job permanent-closure / Sirach useless / Paul now-disclosed) → mystērion's technical hidden-then-revealed grammar → three Gospel kryptō registers (parable ambiguity, structural inevitability, inverted secrecy-as-piety) → Messianic secret as a lexically separate, failing mechanism → hiding-from-God as a different valence of "revealed" (capture, not gift) → Col 3:3-4's settled-but-still-future case and its hedge → the patristic layer's three-way split (Irenaeus/Imitation/Justin). The survey does not build toward a resolution — it builds toward its own closing question.

## Hinge
Is hiddenness a *stage* (temporary, always already leaning toward disclosure) or a *state* (a permanent partition, structurally unrelated to whether any given secret ever crosses over)? The survey's own strongest material argues both ways with the same vocabulary — sathar/galah do jurisdictional work in Deut 29:29/Prov 25:2 and narrative-arc work in Daniel 2/Colossians 1. The galah double-life (reveal = uncover = exile/nakedness-exposure) complicates the arc reading further: even "revealed" isn't uniformly gift-language.

## Register map used for structure
1. Jurisdictional/legal (Deut 29:29, Prov 25:2) — states, not stories; concealment as virtue-in-itself
2. Apocalyptic/mystery-unveiling (Daniel 2, mystērion in Paul) — hidden content now disclosed by event
3. Wisdom-literature disagreement (Job 28 / Sirach / Paul) — permanently closed vs. useless-if-hidden vs. now-ended
4. Parable/pedagogical (Luke 8:10) — one speech act, opposite effects by design
5. Ethical/light — structural inevitability of disclosure (the four-way "nothing hidden" cluster, Matt 5:14)
6. Piety/secrecy-as-virtue (Matt 6) — inverse-value register, God's seeing not God's revealing
7. Hiding-from-God (Gen 3, Ps 139, Jer 23:24, Amos 9:3) — disclosure as capture, not gift
8. Messianic secret — lexically separate (imperative, not kryptō), fails structurally
9. Christic settled-state (Col 3:3-4) — static language still future-oriented
10. Patristic three-way split — Irenaeus (weapon), Imitation (permanent limit), Justin (typological latency)

Consolidated into 7 numbered sections + coda for the composed piece (some registers merged: 1 opens, 2+3 merged into IV, 4+5+6 merged into V, 7+8 merged into VI, 9 folded into end of V, 10 became VII).

## Closing question (reopened, not answered)
"Is 'hidden,' anywhere in this material, ever a permanent category — or does every real instance, except the hiding that fails outright, carry a built-in trajectory toward disclosure?" — restated near-verbatim from the survey as the coda's final line.

## Gem-extraction candidates (5–10 required; used vs. cut)
1. **English "discovery"/dis-cover etymology, incl. legal discovery** — USED (section II). Exact structural match to galah/apokalyptō, low risk, no overreach.
2. **Black hole information paradox (Page curve / islands)** — USED (coda). Structurally identical open question to the survey's own closing question — hidden-permanently vs. hidden-with-eventual-leakage. Kept deliberately unresolved/hedged to match its real state in physics.
3. **Streisand effect (2003 lawsuit backfire)** — USED (section VI). Exact mechanism match to Mark 7:36's "the more He ordered them, the more widely they proclaimed it."
4. **"Watching eyes" honesty-box study (Bateson, Nettle & Roberts 2006)** — USED (section V). Strong mechanism echo for Matt 6's secret-piety-seen-by-God-alone register; flagged explicitly as illustration, not a textual claim.
5. **Zero-knowledge proofs (cryptography)** — CUT. Interesting third-option (verified-without-disclosed) but needed too much setup to land cleanly; risked overclaiming a technical/theological parallel. "A weld that needs a paragraph of setup isn't a weld."
6. **Gödel's incompleteness theorem** — CUT. Tempting for Job 28's permanent inaccessibility, but not an exact mechanism match (Gödel is about unprovability within a system, not about an object simply being out of reach) — vibes-matched rather than exact.
7. **Photographic development (latent image already present, chemically revealed)** — CUT. Nice image for mystērion's "hidden for ages, now revealed" grammar, but redundant once the black hole weld was chosen for the coda and risked diluting focus in section IV, which is already dense.
8. **Sealed/declassified government records (25/50-year rules, some sealed permanently)** — CUT. Good match for the permanent-partition side but largely duplicates what Deut 29:29 and Job 28 already do untranslated; didn't add enough beyond the corpus's own material to earn its word count.
