# Attributions

bible-mcp assembles the following sources. Deep thanks to every project below —
this instrument is a compilation of their generosity.

- **Berean Standard Bible** — Berean Bible Translation Committee, https://berean.bible. Public Domain (dedicated 2023).
- **World English Bible, Ecumenical edition** — ed. Michael Paul Johnson, https://ebible.org/eng-web/. Public Domain. "World English Bible" is a trademark of eBible.org; the text itself is dedicated to the public domain.
- **MACULA Greek (Nestle1904) and MACULA Hebrew (WLC)** — Clear-Bible / Biblica, https://github.com/Clear-Bible. CC BY 4.0. Word-level lemmas, Strong's numbers, morphology, glosses, and semantic domains.
- **OpenBible.info cross-references** — https://www.openbible.info/labs/cross-references/. CC BY. ~345,000 community-voted cross-references.
- **Theographic Bible Metadata** — https://github.com/robertrouse/theographic-bible-metadata. CC BY-SA 4.0. People, places, events, and verse links. This subset remains CC BY-SA within the database.
- **Project Gutenberg** — https://www.gutenberg.org. Public Domain texts: Augustine's Confessions (#3296, tr. Pusey), The Imitation of Christ (#1653, tr. Benham), The Pilgrim's Progress (#131), The Practice of the Presence of God (#5657), Revelations of Divine Love (#52958, ed. Warrack), Orthodoxy (#16769), The Apostolic Fathers (#77576, Roberts-Donaldson-Crombie, Ante-Nicene Fathers). Project Gutenberg boilerplate removed per their trademark license; the texts are public domain.
- **Wikisource** — https://en.wikisource.org. Community transcription of Ante-Nicene Fathers Vol. I (Roberts & Rambaut translations, Public Domain): Irenaeus, Against Heresies I–V and Fragments; Justin Martyr, First Apology, Second Apology, Dialogue with Trypho, Martyrdom of Justin.
- **STEPBible / Tyndale House** — the versification methodology conversation; bible-mcp's versemap is derived empirically in-corpus but keeps a TVTMS-compatible schema in anticipation of their CC BY data.
- **BAAI bge-small-en-v1.5** — embedding model (MIT), run locally via fastembed.

Scripture quotations in generated outputs are from the BSB and WEB as tagged per passage.
